Summary of changes (revision of 2025.findings-emnlp.706)

The evaluation artifacts behind the originally published results could not be
recovered, and those results could not be reproduced. Following the advice of
the EMNLP 2025 programme chairs, all results are replaced by results
reconstructed from the public repository (https://github.com/hailaykidu/MoVoC).
The method is unchanged. A correction notice follows the abstract, and
Appendix D lists every change.

- Evaluation sets (new Section 5.3): part of each in-language evaluation set
  consisted of words whose morpheme boundaries had constrained MoVoC-Tok's own
  training, which the BPE and WordPiece baselines never saw. All in-language
  results are now scored on held-out words only: 76,488 of 81,224 Amharic words
  and 2,438 of 5,224 Tigrinya words. Tigre (1,974) and Ge'ez (172) have no
  training morpheme resource and are unaffected.
- Tables 2 and 4 (intrinsic): new values for BPE, WordPiece and MoVoC-Tok on the
  held-out evaluation sets. MoVoC-Tok scores highest on MorphScore in three of
  four languages and on boundary precision in two of four, not all four. Its
  largest gains are on Tigre, which is evaluated cross-lingually. Rényi entropy
  is reported in raw nats. A new table reports bootstrap confidence intervals
  for the differences against BPE.
- Table 3 (translation): withdrawn. The three-seed reconstruction that replaced
  it in the first corrected version is also withdrawn, together with the OPUS
  appendix table. The decode outputs are not in the repository, the recorded
  hypothesis/reference length ratios (2.06-15.10) show generation failing to
  terminate, and the decoding configuration and model initialisation of those
  runs cannot be established from the surviving records. This version reports
  no translation results and makes no extrinsic claim.
- Table 1, Section 7 and Table 6: the examples are replaced by actual tokenizer
  output and by records from the released data. The translation example is
  withdrawn.
- Sections 4 and 5: training configuration, evaluation data, vocabulary sizes
  (Table 5) and metric definitions are corrected to match the repository.
- Abstract, Conclusion and Limitations are updated to match.
